# Privacy Policy for G-Star FF

**Last updated:** 21 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **G-Star FF** (`com.bidding.gstar`) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Android application.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.

## 1. Who we are

G-Star FF is a results chart application. Users can view daily chart entries. Authorized administrators can sign in to add or update those entries.

**Contact:** G-StarFF@gmail.com

## 2. Information we collect

### Information you provide

- **Administrator credentials.** If you use Admin sign-in, we collect the username and password you enter so we can verify access and, if you choose, update the admin password.
- **Chart entries.** Administrators may submit date, time, and numeric result values (patti / single slots). These values are stored so the public chart can be displayed.

Ordinary chart viewers are not asked to create an account or provide a name, email address, or phone number.

### Information collected automatically

- **Network status.** The app checks whether the device is online before loading or saving chart data.
- **Local session.** After a successful admin login, the app stores a login flag and timestamp on the device so the admin session can last up to 12 hours.
- **App and Firebase diagnostics.** Cloud Firestore may receive technical information needed to deliver and secure the database service (for example IP address, device identifiers used by Google services, and request metadata). We do not use a separate analytics or advertising SDK.

### Information we do not collect

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos, SMS, or call logs. It does not use Google Play Billing and does not process payment card data.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

- Display the results chart
- Authenticate administrators
- Save and update chart entries
- Keep an admin session on the device for a limited time
- Operate, maintain, and fix the app

We do not use the app to show personalized ads, and we do not sell personal information.

## 4. Permissions

The app uses:

- **Internet** — to load and save chart data from Cloud Firestore
- **Network state** — to detect whether the device is connected

These permissions are used only for the purposes above.

## 5. Third-party services

Chart data and administrator credentials are stored with **Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore**. Google may process that data according to its own policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

We do not integrate advertising networks, social login, or payment processors.

## 6. Data storage and retention

- Chart entries remain in Firestore until an administrator changes or deletes them.
- Administrator credentials remain in Firestore until they are changed or the account document is removed.
- The on-device admin session is stored in app preferences and expires after 12 hours, or sooner if the app data is cleared.

You may also delete the app, which removes local session data from your device. It does not automatically delete chart data stored on the server.

## 7. Data sharing

We share information only:

- With Google Firebase, as required to host the database
- If required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or integrity of the app and its users

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

## 8. Children’s privacy

G-Star FF is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Data security

We use industry-standard cloud infrastructure (Firebase) and limit admin features to signed-in administrators. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please use a strong unique admin password and do not share it.

## 10. Your choices

- View chart data without creating an account
- Administrators can change the stored password inside the app
- Uninstall the app or clear app storage to remove the local session
- Email G-StarFF@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of administrator or chart data we hold

If you are in a region with additional privacy rights (including the EEA, UK, or California), contact us to exercise those rights.

## 11. International processing

Firebase may store and process data on servers outside your country. By using the app, you understand that your information may be transferred to those locations.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
